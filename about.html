About Angels Mission

Inspiring Hearts Through Music & Ministry

Our Story

Founded in the heart of Ssenge Wakiso, Njovu Estate, Angels Mission Legacy Organisation (AMLO) emerged from a shared vision to transform lives through the power of music and ministry.

Our journey began with a simple yet profound belief: that music has the extraordinary ability to heal, inspire, and unite people across all boundaries. What started as a small group of passionate individuals has grown into a dynamic force for positive change in Uganda and beyond.

From our humble beginnings in 2020, we have witnessed God's faithfulness in expanding our reach and deepening our impact. Our founding members came together with diverse musical backgrounds but shared a common calling - to use their voices as instruments of worship, healing, and transformation. Through countless hours of practice, prayer, and preparation, we developed our distinctive sound that blends traditional Ugandan musical elements with contemporary worship styles.

Our ministry has taken us from intimate church gatherings to large community events, from wedding celebrations to corporate functions. Each performance is carefully crafted not just to entertain, but to minister to hearts, encourage the weary, and draw people closer to God's love. We believe that every song we sing has the potential to change a life, mend a broken heart, or ignite hope in someone's darkest moment.

As we continue to grow, our commitment remains unwavering: to be faithful stewards of the gifts God has given us, to serve our community with excellence, and to leave a lasting legacy of faith, hope, and love. Every performance we deliver carries with it the hopes and dreams of our community. We are more than just a singing group - we are ambassadors of hope, messengers of love, and instruments of divine purpose.
